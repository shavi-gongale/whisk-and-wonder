Design a premium, award-winning luxury bakery website called "Whisk & Wonder".

The website should not feel like a typical bakery e-commerce site. Instead, it should feel like entering a magical luxury dessert boutique where every interaction is elegant, immersive, and delightful.

The overall experience should combine the sophistication of luxury brands with the warmth and charm of a cozy artisan bakery.

The design should be Awwwards-inspired, Apple-level polished, minimal, cinematic, cute, premium, and portfolio-worthy.

Overall Style

Create a website that feels like:

Apple website polish
Luxury boutique branding
Soft pastel aesthetic
Japanese artisan bakery
Scandinavian interior design
Disney-like magical atmosphere
Awwwards-winning interactive experience

The UI should feel expensive, elegant, cozy, handcrafted, and unforgettable.

Final Goal

The final website should feel like stepping into a magical luxury bakery, not browsing an ordinary online store.

Every screen should be visually stunning, highly polished, and portfolio-worthy, with a balance of professional elegance and cute charm. The overall quality should resemble work featured on Awwwards, with the clean refinement of Apple's design philosophy, while remaining an original bakery brand experience.
